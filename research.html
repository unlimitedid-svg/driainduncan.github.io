Research themes

Dr Duncan's published research has spanned several themes throughout his career.

Diagnostic ultrasound & musculoskeletal imaging

Pioneering work in carpal tunnel ultrasound, forefoot imaging, ultrasound elastography of the liver and thyroid, and the role of ultrasound-guided injection therapy in musculoskeletal medicine.

Nuclear medicine

Early adoption and clinical evaluation of fusion nuclear medicine (Siemens Symbia SPECT-CT), quantitative bone scanning (xSPECT), and the development of Tc-PSMA for prostate cancer staging in Australia.

Regenerative therapies & PRP

Ongoing clinical research and review of platelet-rich plasma therapy in osteoarthritis, tendinopathy, and rotator cuff disease — with particular interest in PRP combined with hyaluronic acid for knee osteoarthritis.

Rheumatology & bone disease

Earlier work on erosive osteoarthritis in chronic renal failure, hand OA, and the role of bone scans in rheumatic disease.
